Use Attack Move range to set when your champion can attack. If a selected target is too far away, your champion follows it until in range, then attempts the attack. Stop or right-click to cancel the chase. Toggle the range indicator on or off. Use Training bots to set 1–8 dummies and Bot durability to choose 10–200 hits per dummy. Bullet hell difficulty adjusts wave size, firing frequency, and projectile speed from Easy to Extreme. Target abilities / skillshots fires one aimed projectile per volley; triple-shot spreads are disabled. Mixed combines those shots with bullet-hell edge waves. Adjust Skillshot speed to practice slower or faster projectiles: around 1200 for Morgana/Lux Q or 2000 for Ezreal Q. The same slider also changes bullet-hell projectile speed; bullet hell still sends dense waves from the screen edges. Use Edge camera pan speed and Camera zoom sliders to tune camera movement and view distance. The arena is now one continuous map instead of repeating 3×3 tiles. Hold Space to keep the camera centered on your champion; release it to return to edge-scrolling.

Attack Move selects the living target nearest your cursor within a generous 650-unit radius. If no target is within that radius, it moves toward the cursor using the red Attack Move indicator. Aim accuracy is based on cursor-to-target distance: directly on or close to a target scores about 95–100%, and falls gradually farther away. A selected target takes one hit per attack; accuracy measures cursor precision, not whether the attack connects.
